An Excel-based retail sales analytics project designed to analyze sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, and store performance through an interactive dashboard.

## 📌 Project Overview

The TrendMart Retail Sales Dashboard is an interactive data analytics project developed using Microsoft Excel.

The dashboard transforms retail sales data into meaningful business insights by analyzing sales, profit, quantity, orders, returns, regions, states, categories, customer membership, payment methods, sales channels, discounts, products, brands, and store performance.

It helps identify business trends, evaluate profitability, monitor performance, and support data-driven decision-making.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall retail sales, profit, quantity, and order performance.
- Identify monthly sales and profit trends.
- Compare sales performance across regions, states, categories, and stores.
- Evaluate discount and return patterns.
- Analyze customer membership and purchasing behavior.
- Identify high-sales, low-profit products.
- Compare brand profitability and store performance.
- Develop an interactive dashboard for business decision-making.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

- **Sales Performance:** TrendMart generated total sales of approximately ₹92.27 Lakhs.
- **Profitability:** The business recorded a total profit of approximately ₹18.05 Lakhs with a reported profit margin of 19.56%.
- **Order Volume:** A total of 3,000 orders were recorded, with 6,118 units sold.
- **Returns:** The overall return rate was 4.87%.
- **Sales Channels:** Offline sales contributed 66% of total sales, while online sales contributed 34%.
- **Customer Membership:** Regular membership contributed 32% of membership sales.
- **Payment Methods:** Credit Card accounted for 22% of sales among the displayed payment modes.
- **Regional Performance:** State-wise sales analysis compares performance across Karnataka, Telangana, Kerala, Tamil Nadu, and Andhra Pradesh.
- **Product Profitability:** High-sales, low-profit products can be identified for further profitability analysis.
- **Business Opportunities:** The dashboard provides insights and actions related to low-margin products, online sales, and return rates.

## 🔍 Analytical Approach

1. **Data Cleaning:** Prepared retail sales data for analysis.
2. **Data Analysis:** Analyzed sales, profit, quantity, orders, returns, and customer behavior.
3. **Pivot Tables:** Summarized data across regions, states, categories, products, and other business dimensions.
4. **Pivot Charts:** Created visualizations to identify trends and compare performance.
5. **Dashboard Development:** Combined KPIs, charts, and interactive filters into a single dashboard.
6. **Business Insights:** Interpreted the results to identify performance trends and potential business opportunities.
